# Dossiers, Datasets, and Diplomacy: How Data Strategy Drives Decisions

Data strategy — including provenance, privacy, and governance — is shaping high-stakes decisions in national security and corporate strategy.

## What happened
Signals from our intel collection indicate increased attention to data lineage and dossier-like datasets influencing policy debates and procurement conversations. The move from siloed data to connected datasets enhances decision integrity but also raises questions about governance boundaries, cross-border data sharing, and transparency.

## Context
Data strategy now sits at the center of governance debates in both the public and private sectors. As organizations adopt more interconnected data ecosystems, the need for provenance standards, privacy safeguards, and auditable data flows becomes critical for credible decision-making.

## What’s driving it
- Incentives: accountability, policy alignment, and investor confidence; constraints: data quality, interoperability, and privacy considerations.

## Implications
- For business: governance and liability considerations; for cybersecurity: data integrity and tamper-evidence; for national security: resilience, transparency, and cross-border data governance.

## What to watch
- Trends in data provenance standards, cross-border data sharing policies, and governance benchmarks for large datasets.
