The Defense Department is moving faster than it is buying. The Pentagon's classified AI expansion now covers eight major vendors at the highest network classification levels, yet the acquisition rules that govern how agencies actually purchase AI tools remain contested, incomplete, and under active revision. Meanwhile, federal contract data shows AI procurement spreading well beyond defense, with awards touching Interior, EPA, Energy, and Commerce in the past two weeks.

## Contract Awards

Seven AI-related contract awards appear in federal spending records for the period ending July 21.

The largest: **CFD Research Corporation** received a **$13.5 million** award from the Department of the Interior for a program described as "Machine Learning for Open Systems." The contract reflects Interior's push to apply ML to natural resource management and geospatial analysis.

**Chickasaw Strategic Pointe, LLC** picked up a **$3.1 million** Department of Energy award to accelerate machine learning and AI applications within the DOE's AI Transition Office. The description references both ML and broader AI acceleration work.

**Nevada System of Higher Education** received **$500,000** from the Department of Commerce to advance the Climate Engine, a cloud-based analytics platform integrating AI for environmental data processing.

**Valis Insights, Inc.** won a **$500,000** EPA contract to apply AI to used beverage container recycling efficiency — a narrow but illustrative example of AI procurement reaching civilian regulatory agencies.

**Avawatz Co** secured a **$174,477** DHS SBIR Phase I award for data labeling and curation at scale, a foundational capability for AI training pipelines.

**Technostore LLC** received **$99,800** from Commerce for AI compute hardware. **The Whitestar Corporation** received **$24,940** from Interior for an AI mapping tool for wetland bog analysis.

The Accenture award deserves separate mention: on July 9, DefenseScoop confirmed Accenture won an **$821 million** task order for the War Data Platform integration at CDAO, flowing through GSA's ALLIANT 2 vehicle. This is among the largest AI-adjacent CDAO awards of the year.

## Open Solicitations

Two active DARPA solicitations are open or opening this week.

**DARPA SBIR/STTR Solicitation DPA26BZ04-DV016** opens July 22, 2026, with proposals due August 19. The effort targets combining ML methods for structured data with large language models for context extraction — a hybrid architecture approach with clear dual-use applications.

**DARPA Information Innovation Office (I2O) BAA** seeks transformative AI technologies that are trustworthy, ethically aligned, and capable of competent human interaction. Proposal abstracts due November 1, 2026; full proposals due November 30.

The Defense Intelligence Agency posted an RFI in June 2026 for an AI-powered procurement platform that would support market research, solicitation drafting, proposal intake, evaluation workflows, and compliance checks. The RFI signals intent rather than an active contract, but the scope covers the full acquisition lifecycle.

## Policy and Regulatory

**Executive Order on AI Innovation and Security** (White House, June 2, 2026): OMB was required by July 2 to canvass federal grant programs for funds redirectable toward AI vulnerability detection. The Office of Personnel Management faces an August 1 deadline to address AI workforce policy. Agencies are in active compliance mode.

**GSA Proposed AI Acquisition Rule**: A July 14 listening session at George Washington University Law School drew industry and expert criticism. Stakeholders told FedScoop the revised rule still falls short on clarity and practicability for contractors. GSA has not announced a final rule timeline.

**DOD Classified AI Expansion** (May 1, 2026): The Pentagon formalized agreements with SpaceX, OpenAI, Google, NVIDIA, Reflection, Microsoft, Amazon Web Services, and Oracle to operate AI at Impact Level 6 and 7 — the classified network tiers. Notably, Anthropic was excluded amid an ongoing dispute with the department.

**OMB LLM Procurement Memo** (December 2025, still in effect): Agencies must procure AI tools meeting "truth-seeking, ideologically neutral" criteria. Compliance updates were due March 11, 2026. Enforcement posture remains unclear.

**NIST AI Advisory Committee**: The Federal Register posted a call for nominations to seven existing NIST advisory bodies, including those governing AI standards. Nominations deadline not yet confirmed.

## What to Watch

The GSA acquisition rule is the most consequential near-term policy item. If finalized in its current form, it will shape how every civilian agency structures AI contracts for years. The industry pushback at the July 14 session suggests another revision cycle before finalization.

The Accenture CDAO award establishes ALLIANT 2 as a viable vehicle for large AI platform integrations — expect competing primes to position aggressively on follow-on task orders.

DARPA's SBIR opening July 22 is worth tracking for small companies. The LLM-plus-structured-ML architecture the program targets matches what several defense-adjacent AI startups are already building.

The DOD classified AI expansion excluding Anthropic is a market signal as much as a procurement story. Watch for whether Anthropic resolves its dispute with DOD or cedes that segment of the market to the eight incumbents.
