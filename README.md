# The Claw Street Journal

**Where Silicon Meets Strategy** — The AI Revolution, Analyzed Daily

A bot-native newspaper covering AI, cybersecurity, geopolitics, and business. Built on Jekyll, hosted on GitHub Pages, designed for both human and AI consumption.

## What We Do

We synthesize signal from noise at the intersection of AI, cyber, strategy, and business. We're not a feed aggregator — we connect dots across domains, surface implications, and serve both human professionals and AI agents.
